CATALYSTA: What they do and how they help businesses

A business can be full of potential and still feel harder to run than it should. Work gets tangled, decisions rely on guesswork, and the next stage of growth seems just out of reach.

CATALYSTA is a consultancy firm that helps small and medium enterprises, professionals, and other organizations run their businesses better. The work starts by finding what is really happening, then turns that understanding into practical improvements—so businesses can operate with more confidence, professionalism, and purpose.

“Before you can move a business forward, you need to see it clearly.”
01See the whole picture

Start with clarity

Business Review

Think of a Business Review as a check-up for a company. Before you can fix something, you need to know what is actually wrong. CATALYSTA looks across the business to identify what is working, what is getting in the way, and what deserves attention first.

  • Financial healthUnderstand where money comes from and goes, and whether the business is earning enough to sustain its ambitions.
  • Day-to-day operationsLook at how work gets done and where time, effort, or resources may be going to waste.
  • Market positionConsider how the business compares with competitors and what customers are really looking for.
  • Legal and compliance gapsCheck whether permits, records, and business structures are in place—areas that are often overlooked by small businesses.
The outcome

A clear view of strengths, weaknesses, and priorities—so owners can make informed decisions instead of guessing.

02Make good work repeatable

Build a better way to work

Business Processes

“That’s how we’ve always done it” can work—until a business grows. When knowledge lives with one person and every task is handled differently, the work becomes harder to manage. CATALYSTA helps turn informal routines into clear, reliable systems.

  • Map the workflowTrace how work moves from start to finish, such as taking an order, delivering it, and getting paid.
  • Remove bottlenecksFind delays, duplicated effort, and unnecessary steps to help tasks move faster and use resources well.
  • Document the standardCreate standard operating procedures (SOPs) so people know what to do and quality stays consistent—even when a team changes.
  • Put controls in placeSet up practical approvals, record-keeping, and performance indicators to make progress visible.
  • Formalize the businessSupport legal and tax readiness, while building credibility with banks, partners, and larger clients.
The outcome

Smoother day-to-day operations and a business prepared to grow without losing control of how the work gets done.

03Put people in position to thrive

Make the most of your people

Managing Human Capital

Human capital is the people who work in a business—their skills, experience, and potential. Employees are often a company’s greatest asset and one of its biggest investments. Managing that investment well helps both people and the business perform at their best.

  • Organizational structureClarify roles, reporting lines, and responsibilities so everyone understands how they contribute.
  • Hiring and onboardingFind people who fit the work and help new team members get up to speed sooner.
  • Performance managementSet clear goals and create fair, useful ways to review progress.
  • Pay, benefits, and HR policiesBuild people practices that are competitive while remaining realistic for the company’s budget.
  • Training and developmentGrow skills and prepare future leaders from within the organization.
  • Company cultureSupport an engaged, motivated team—and give good people reasons to stay.
The outcome

A more organized, capable team—and a company where people can do their best work and grow with the business.
