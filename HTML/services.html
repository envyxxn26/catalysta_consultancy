From Groundwork to Growth

CATALYSTA: What they do and how they help businesses
Good growth is built from the inside out. CATALYSTA helps SMEs, professionals, and organizations understand what is holding them back—and put the right foundations in place to move forward.

Explore the three disciplines ↓

Business Review

Think of a Business Review as a check-up for a company. Before you can fix something, you need to know what is actually wrong. CATALYSTA looks across the business to identify what is working, what is getting in the way, and what deserves attention first.

01Financial health

Understand where money comes from and goes, and whether the business is earning enough to sustain its ambitions.

02Day-to-day operations

Look at how work gets done and where time, effort, or resources may be going to waste.

03Market position

Consider how the business compares with competitors and what customers are really looking for.

04Legal and compliance gaps

Check whether permits, records, and business structures are in place—areas that are often overlooked by small businesses

Business Proposal

“That’s how we’ve always done it” can work—until a business grows. When knowledge lives with one person and every task is handled differently, the work becomes harder to manage. CATALYSTA helps turn informal routines into clear, reliable systems.

01Map the workflow

Trace how work moves from start to finish, such as taking an order, delivering it, and getting paid.

02Remove bottlenecks

Find delays, duplicated effort, and unnecessary steps to help tasks move faster and use resources well.

03Document the standard

Create standard operating procedures (SOPs) so people know what to do and quality stays consistent.

04Put controls in place

Set up practical approvals, record-keeping, and performance indicators to make progress visible.

05Formalize the business

Support legal and tax readiness, while building credibility with banks, partners, and larger clients.

Managing Human Capital

Human capital is the people who work in a business—their skills, experience, and potential. Employees are often a company’s greatest asset and one of its biggest investments. Managing that investment well helps both people and the business perform at their best.

01Organizational structure

Clarify roles, reporting lines, and responsibilities so everyone understands how they contribute.

02Hiring and onboarding

Find people who fit the work and help new team members get up to speed sooner.

03Performance management

Set clear goals and create fair, useful ways to review progress.

04Pay, benefits, and HR policies

Build people practices that are competitive while remaining realistic for the company’s budget.

05Training and development

Grow skills and prepare future leaders from within the organization.

06Company culture

Support an engaged, motivated team—and give good people reasons to stay.
